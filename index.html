Introduction

Atomic Security (“Atomic”, “we”, “our”, or “the Bot”) is a Discord security bot designed to help server administrators detect suspicious activity, prevent abuse, and protect their communities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Atomic may process, why it is processed, how it is stored, and what choices are available to users and server administrators.

We aim to collect only the information necessary to provide and maintain the Bot's security features.

2

Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled and the server's configuration, Atomic may process the following information:

  • Discord identifiers: server IDs, user IDs, channel IDs, and other identifiers required for the Bot to function.
  • Server configuration: security settings, configured honeypot channels, logging channels, punishment settings, trusted users, and related preferences.
  • Security events: detected spam, suspicious links, repeated messages, suspected raids, honeypot triggers, moderation actions, and relevant error or incident records.
  • Message information: message content or links may be inspected when necessary to detect spam, scams, or suspicious activity. Whether any content is retained depends on the implemented feature.
  • Operational information: error logs and technical information needed to diagnose failures and maintain the Bot.

Atomic does not need Discord passwords or users' account login credentials to provide its ordinary security features.

3

Why We Process Information

Information may be processed to:

  • Detect spam, scams, suspicious links, raids, and other potentially harmful activity.
  • Apply security actions configured by authorized server administrators.
  • Maintain security logs and investigate incidents.
  • Store server-specific settings and trusted-user configurations.
  • Diagnose technical problems, prevent abuse, and improve reliability.
  • Respond to privacy requests and meet applicable legal obligations.

Information will not be used for unrelated purposes without an appropriate legal basis and any required additional notice.

4

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, personal data is processed on a legal basis permitted by applicable data protection law.

Depending on the specific processing activity, this may include legitimate interests in protecting Discord communities and maintaining service security, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where consent is the appropriate basis.

The applicable legal basis must be determined for each relevant processing activity. Where legitimate interests are relied upon, those interests and the necessary balancing assessment should be documented.

Automated Security Actions

Depending on server configuration, Atomic may automatically flag suspicious activity or carry out actions such as warnings, kicks, bans, or other moderation measures.

These actions are intended to protect servers, but automated detection can make mistakes. Server administrators should configure security settings carefully and provide an appropriate way to review disputed actions.

Where legally required, information about relevant automated decision-making, its consequences, and available rights will be provided. Under EU data protection law, individuals have rights relating to automated decision-making and profiling. [1][2]

8

Data Security

We aim to protect stored information through reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the service.

Access to configuration and security records should be restricted to authorized persons. Bot tokens, API keys, and other secrets must be kept private and must not be published in source code or public repositories.

No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, individuals may have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal data.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion or restriction of processing.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Request data portability where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

GDPR rights include the rights to be informed, access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, objection, and rights relating to automated decision-making. [1][2][10]

Server Administrators' Responsibilities

Server administrators are responsible for configuring Atomic appropriately for their communities and ensuring that their own use of security logs and moderation features complies with applicable rules and laws.

Administrators should avoid collecting or sharing more information than necessary and should restrict access to private security logs.

This section does not remove any legal responsibilities that apply to Atomic or its operator.

11

Children's Privacy

Atomic is a Discord security tool and is not intended to collect personal information from children for unrelated purposes.

Information may nevertheless be processed when users participate in Discord servers protected by the Bot. Such processing must be limited to legitimate, necessary security purposes and comply with applicable data protection requirements.
